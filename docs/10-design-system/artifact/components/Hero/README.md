# Hero

The home hero: a display headline (max 18ch), a two-line lede, one primary action and one quiet link, the SDC mark art on the opposite side, and the stats row underneath.

- One `t-display` per site. Text at the inline-start; art at the inline-end; on phones the art follows the actions at 60% width.
- The art uses the existing files (`hero-mark-dark.png` / `hero-mark-light.png`), swapped by theme; it is decorative (`alt=""`).
- Headline, lede and figures here are placeholders.

Full spec: `docs/10-design-system/components.md` in the repo.
